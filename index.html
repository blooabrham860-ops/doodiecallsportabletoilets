Frequently Asked Questions

1. How many units do I need for my event?

The number of units depends on the expected attendance and the duration of the event. Generally, we recommend one unit for every 50 to 75 people for an eight-hour event. If food and beverages are served, you may need to increase the count to ensure a positive guest experience.

2. What is included in a standard service visit?

Each service visit includes the thorough cleaning of the interior, waste evacuation, and the replenishment of toilet paper and hand sanitizer. We also inspect the unit for any structural issues to ensure it remains safe for use. Regular servicing is key to maintaining a fresh and hygienic environment for all users.

3. Can units be placed on uneven ground?

For safety and functionality, we prefer to place units on a flat, level surface such as asphalt, concrete, or firm turf. If your site has uneven terrain, our drivers carry leveling equipment to ensure the unit is stable and the door functions correctly. We will work with you during delivery to find the safest possible location.

4. How far in advance should I book my rental?

We suggest booking your units at least two weeks in advance for standard projects and several months in advance for large festivals or peak wedding season. However, we understand that emergencies happen and often accommodate last-minute requests for a porta-potty rental in #State whenever inventory allows.

5. Does the rental price include delivery and pickup?

Yes, our quotes typically include the cost of delivery, setup, a set number of service visits, and final removal. We provide transparent pricing so you can stay within your budget while securing high-quality porta-potties for rent in #State. Contact us for a detailed estimate tailored to your specific needs.
